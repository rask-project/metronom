Rask Metronom 3.0.0 looks native on macOS and reorganises the layout for phones in landscape.

### macOS

- **Native look:** buttons, switches, the BPM wheel and dialogs now follow the macOS style instead of Material.
- Light and dark themes apply to the native controls too, with the app's orange accent.
- Dialogs have a border and a shadow over a slightly dimmed page, so they stand out in both themes.

### Android

- **Landscape layout:** the metronome takes the left side and the note values, BPM and play controls stack on the
  right. The BPM wheel shows fewer rows when the screen is short.
- Dialogs fit between the system bars, and the settings scroll when they do not fit.
- Fixed the note value names being hard to read on the dark theme.

### Also

- Your light or dark choice is kept from the previous version.
