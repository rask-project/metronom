Rask Metronom 2.0.0 adds time signatures and makes subdivisions follow the beat, like a real metronome.

### Time signatures

- **Choose the time signature** with the button next to play: 1 to 12 beats over a half, quarter or eighth note.
- **Compound meters** (6/8, 9/8, 12/8) are counted in dotted quarters, each split in three eighths, and the BPM
  counts the dotted quarter.
- Changes made while playing start on the next beat, so the count never breaks.

### Subdivisions

- The note value now divides **each beat**: in 4/4, eighths play 2 notes per beat, sixteenths 4 and thirty-seconds 8.
- Only the note values that fit the beat are offered. Half notes are available in x/2 and dotted quarters in
  compound meters.
- **Three clicks:** an accent on the first beat of the bar, a medium click on the other beats and a soft click on
  the subdivisions.
- **The beat bar** shows one group per beat with a cell for each note, so you can see how many notes to play in
  that beat.
- **The pendulum** swings once per beat instead of on every click.
- Note icons use filled note heads, as in standard notation.

### Also

- Your time signature is remembered with the BPM and note value.
- macOS: the translucent window follows the app's light or dark theme instead of the system's.
