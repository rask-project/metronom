## What's new

Metronom 3.2.0 gets the Android app ready for the Play Store: it keeps playing in the background and fits
every screen.

- **Plays in the background:** on Android the metronome keeps going when you switch apps or turn the screen off.
  Its notification shows the tempo and the time signature, with the note value as its icon, and has Stop and
  Resume; tapping it brings the app back where you left it. Closing the app from the recent apps stops it.
- **Fits the screen:** on a short phone the metronome sits beside the play button and the beat bar, on a taller
  one it stands above them, and in landscape the controls move to the right. Nothing hides under the status or
  navigation bars any more.
- **Beat bar:** it now sits under the play and time signature buttons, and its marks shrink instead of
  disappearing in long bars such as 12/4 or with thirty-second notes.
- **New app ID:** the Android package is now `io.github.rask_project.metronom`. It installs as a new app; an
  earlier APK can be uninstalled.
- **Privacy policy:** the website has a [privacy policy](https://rask-project.github.io/metronom/privacy/) —
  Metronom collects no data.
