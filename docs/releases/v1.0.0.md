## What's new

Rask Metronom 1.0.0 is the first release: a metronome for macOS, Linux and Android.

### Metronome

- **Sample-accurate timing.** Clicks are scheduled against the audio clock, so the beat never drifts.
- **BPM control** with the classic tempo markings, from Largo to Prestissimo.
- **Note values:** quarter, eighth, sixteenth and thirty-second notes, with an accented downbeat.
- **Automatic BPM increase** at an interval you choose, for practising a passage faster and faster.
- **Audio output selection** in Settings.
- The screen stays awake while the metronome plays.

### Keyboard

- **Space** plays and stops.
- **↑ / ↓** change the BPM by one.
- **Page Up / Page Down** jump to the next or previous tempo marking.
- **⌘,** opens Settings on macOS.

### macOS

- **Native window:** translucent and blurred, with the content extending under the title bar.
- **Rask Metronom ▸ About** shows the version and the Qt it runs on.
- **Automatic updates.** Once a day the app looks for a newer release, downloads it in the background and installs
  it when you quit. **Rask Metronom ▸ Check for Updates…** checks right away. Updates are signed and open without
  clearing the quarantine flag.

### Everywhere

- Light, dark or system theme.
- Layout adapts to portrait and landscape.
- English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, French and Italian.
- Your BPM, note value, theme and language are remembered.
